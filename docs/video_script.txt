ArcTreasury Copilot is an AI-powered treasury operations platform that automates USDC payouts across multiple blockchains using Circle's developer tools and Arc Testnet.

Today's treasury teams face a real problem. Moving USDC globally is manual, slow, and error-prone. There are no built-in risk controls, idle funds earn zero yield, and cross-chain movement requires juggling multiple tools. We built ArcTreasury Copilot to solve all of this in one unified system.

Let's start with the policy engine. Every payout goes through five rules before it moves a single dollar. First, we check the amount against configurable limits. Then we validate the destination chain is supported. Next, we score the recipient address against blocklists and known-good registries. We run a velocity check to flag unusual payout frequency. And finally, we detect duplicate recipients within the same batch. Each rule contributes a weighted risk score from zero to one hundred, which maps to one of five decisions: Approved, Queued, Review, Held, or Rejected. Every single decision is logged to a full audit trail.

Now here's where it gets powerful. Our Treasury Copilot Agent runs autonomously in the background. It creates batches, runs the policy engine, auto-approves items based on your chosen strategy, and executes payouts, all without human intervention. You can choose from three strategies. Conservative mode only auto-approves low-risk queued items. Balanced mode handles both queued and review items under a risk threshold of fifty. Aggressive mode auto-approves nearly everything, including held items. There's also a one-click AutoPilot button that runs the entire pipeline end to end in about ten seconds.

For multi-chain settlement, we implemented Circle's Cross-Chain Transfer Protocol V2. We support seven chains: Ethereum, Polygon, Arbitrum, Base, Avalanche, Solana, and Arc. That gives us forty-two directional bridge routes. Each cross-chain transfer follows the burn, attest, mint flow, and Arc-native routes get reduced fees.

But what about idle USDC sitting in the treasury? That's where our RWA-backed reserves come in. We offer four tokenized asset classes. US Treasury Bills at five point two five percent APY. A USDC Money Market Fund at four point eight percent. Investment-grade corporate bonds at six point one percent. And a Tokenized Real Estate Fund at seven point five percent. The system continuously monitors treasury health across three dimensions: liquidity ratio, diversification index, and weighted yield, producing a composite letter grade that drives auto-rebalancing.

For getting money in and out, Circle Gateway provides fiat on and off ramp functionality. We support Wire, ACH, and SEPA payment rails across four currencies: US Dollar, Euro, British Pound, and Singapore Dollar. Non-USD deposits are automatically converted through live FX rates before minting USDC, and the UI shows a real-time conversion preview.

And this isn't just a prototype. We completed four real USDC transfers on Arc Testnet using Circle Programmable Wallets. A real wallet was funded, real transactions were executed, and real transaction hashes are verifiable on-chain. Every simulated element in the UI is clearly labeled so you always know what's real.

The entire system runs on FastAPI with nine database tables, forty-three API endpoints, and a React frontend with seven pages. Every state transition, from batch creation to policy decisions to agent actions, is captured in a comprehensive audit log.

ArcTreasury Copilot shows how on-chain infrastructure can power real-world financial operations today. Automated, compliant, and yield-generating. Thank you.
